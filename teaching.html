Teaching

I enjoy fostering a welcoming environment in the classroom, and structuring incentives around students actually learning the material instead of producing a polished-looking answer. I have a lot to learn and I'm always trying to become a better instructor, but something I'm very proud of is building courses around student projects, whether that means using probability to analyze silly roulette strategies, or using computational social choice to investigate minority representation in their home state.
